Use `scan.py --compose-colored --compose-replaceability --compose-cp92 --compose-cp93`.

The new opt-in layer pins the consolidated CP93 strict inverse implementation and
its manifest, and applies its full-file inverse only to the exact six-file
intersection with this historical audit: BlockVolcano, MachineFan, PistonInserter,
RenderExcavator, RenderRadarScreen and TileEntityCraneConsole. The first three
belong to normal-cube queries, the next two to normal-bearing renderer streams,
and the last to World air queries. Eighteen unrelated-edit/truncation/line-ending
mutants are rejected by this outer inverse.

CP93 is reversed first. The existing CP92, colored and replaceability inverses
then restore their original inputs; CP92 prevalidation explicitly receives the
CP93-restored text. All 518 historical path/line/role/source rows and every
historical source/artifact hash remain unchanged. No historical manifest, source
hash, occurrence TSV, README or bytecode evidence is repinned. No new runtime or
cache-observability claim is made.
